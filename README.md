# AgentTools

Save your Ingress agent stats and medals and see them in one place: share them from Ingress with
the AgentTools Android app, and see them in the app and on
[agent-tools.elkuku.dev](https://agent-tools.elkuku.dev).

## 📖 [Read the documentation](https://elkuku.github.io/AgentTools/)

How to install the app, sign in and save your stats and medals, with screenshots of the app and
the website. It's available in English, German and Spanish.

- [Download the app](https://github.com/elkuku/AgentTools/releases/latest) (Android 8.0 or newer)
- [Developers](docs/developers/index.md)
