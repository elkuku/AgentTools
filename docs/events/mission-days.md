# Ingress Mission Days

Mission Days from the quarterly lists on [ingress.com/news](https://ingress.com/news), as of 4 November 2026.

**235 Mission Days**, from April 2022 to December 2026. Dates after 4 November 2026 are still upcoming.

See also: [XM Anomalies](./anomalies).

## Coverage

- **Official lists exist from Q3 2022.** Everything from there on comes from them, except the gaps below.
- **Before Q3 2022:** no list was found. Mission Days began around 2016, when the medal was introduced, so most early ones are missing here. The only 2022 entries before Q3 are single mentions.
- **Q1 and Q2 2023:** no list page was found. Only Ome is known from a notice. The Echo Anomaly weekends in Q2 had Mission Days too, but their cities are not clear from the rules page.
- **Q3 2025:** standalone Mission Days were paused during the move to Niantic Spatial. Mission Days tied to the +Delta Anomalies went ahead, but no list page was found, so they are not in the table.
- **Cancelled events:** the lists were not always updated afterwards, so a listed event may not have taken place. Valencia on 3 November 2024 is marked because it was officially cancelled.

The cities are written as the lists give them, so spelling varies. The flags show the country.
