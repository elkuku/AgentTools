# Getting started

AgentTools has two parts that share one account:

- the **Android app**, which receives the stats you share from Ingress and saves them, and
- the **website** [agent-tools.elkuku.dev](https://agent-tools.elkuku.dev), where you see the same
  stats, medals, timeline and banners in the browser.

You sign in to both with your Google account.

## Install the app

1. Download the APK from the [latest release](https://github.com/elkuku/AgentTools/releases/latest)
   on your Android phone (Android 8.0 or newer).
2. Open it and allow installing apps from your browser or file manager when Android asks.

There is also a dev build with the latest changes, "AgentTools Dev", which installs next to the
release app: see [Downloads](/downloads).

## Sign in

<img src="/screenshots/app/landing.webp" alt="The app before signing in" class="shot-phone right">

1. Open AgentTools and tap **Sign in** at the top, then pick your Google account.
2. Your account is registered right away; you can upload stats at once.
3. The first stats you share set your **agent name**. From then on only stats of this agent can
   be saved to your account, and an agent name another account already has is refused.
4. Choose what other users see of your profile. Everything is private until you choose: the app
   asks you after signing in, and the website takes you to your profile. See
   [Privacy](/guide/privacy).

The landing page and the settings have a language switch: English, German and Spanish. Medal,
stat and faction names stay in English, as in Ingress.

## Save your first stats

1. In Ingress, open your agent profile, select **ALL TIME** and tap the share button.
2. Choose **AgentTools**. The stats are saved right away, and the app shows what changed since
   your last upload.

Then read on:

- [The Android app](/guide/app): everything the app does, screen by screen.
- [The website](/guide/web): the same on the web, plus importing older stats.
