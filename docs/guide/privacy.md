# Privacy

## What other users see

Your profile is **private until you choose** what other signed-in users see. Choose in the app
(account menu → **Settings → Visible to other users**) or on the website (**Profile**); both
change the same setting.

| Part | What others see |
|---|---|
| **Agent name, faction and level** | Your agent page and your entry in the list of agents. The other parts are only shown with it. |
| **Profile picture** | Your Google profile picture next to your agent name. |
| **Latest stats** | The all-time stats of your newest upload, with medals. |
| **Stats history** | Every upload with its date and AP, and your timeline with your events. |
| **Custom medals** | Your anomaly, annual and event medals. |
| **Banners** | The number of banners you published, have to do and have done, with your lists and the map. |

Unchecked parts stay private. Only signed-in users see agent pages; nothing is public.

## What is stored

- Your Google account's email (as your identifier), its Google ID and your profile picture.
- Your agent name and the stats you upload or import.
- Your custom medals, timeline events and the banners synced from Bannergress.

Screenshots of your medals are read on your phone and never uploaded. The Bannergress sign-in
stays on your phone and is left out of backups.
