# Ingress links

Useful sites for Ingress agents. The same list is in the
[AgentTools website](https://agent-tools.elkuku.dev/links) and the Android app.

To add or change a link, edit
[docs/links.md](https://github.com/elkuku/AgentTools/edit/master/docs/links.md) and open a pull
request. Every link is checked once a day.

## Official

- [Ingress](https://ingress.com) — The official site.
- [Ingress news](https://ingress.com/news) — Announcements, events and updates.
- [Ingress support](https://www.ingress.com/en/support) — Help and contact.
- [Intel Map](https://intel.ingress.com) — The official web map. Needs a sign-in.
- [Ingress on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.nianticproject.ingress) — The Android app.
- [Ingress on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/ingress-prime/id576505181) — The iOS app.

## Portals

- [Wayfarer](https://wayfarer.nianticlabs.com) — Submit and review new portals.

## Maps and tools

- [IITC-CE](https://iitc.app) — The community edition of the Intel map, with plugins.
- [Bannergress](https://bannergress.com) — Find mission banners and keep your to-do and done lists.
- [Agent Stats](https://www.agent-stats.com) — Stats and leaderboards for agents and groups.

## Events and community

- [Fevgames](https://fevgames.net) — Events, First Saturday, medals and guides.
- [Ingress wiki](https://ingress.fandom.com) — The community wiki.
- [r/Ingress](https://www.reddit.com/r/Ingress/) — The Ingress subreddit.

## Telegram

- [Ingress](https://t.me/NianticOfficial) — The official Ingress channel.
- [Ingress Updates](https://t.me/IUENG) — Cross-faction news: app updates, anomalies and rumors.
- [IITC News](https://t.me/iitc_news) — News about IITC-CE and its plugins.
- [IITC chat](https://t.me/+1xncfR_TV3BjZTIy) — The IITC-CE community chat.
